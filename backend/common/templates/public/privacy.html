What Choralation uses, what others can see, and how to contact us about your data.

Effective and last updated: 26 October 2026.

Who operates the service

Choralation is operated by Toufic Lattouf in Lebanon. For privacy, security, access, correction or deletion requests, email tofi.lattouf@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days after receiving a request and may need to verify that you own the account.

Information we handle

We store your username, email address, password hash, verification state and account permissions. We also store choir memberships and requests, choir settings, Mass dates and programs, hymn selections, and the hymn metadata, lyrics and PDFs that users provide. Choir participation and programs can reveal religious activity; add only information you are comfortable sharing with the audiences described below.

We use account-link token hashes, expiration and use records, session information, and hashed rate-limit identifiers for security. Infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request paths, timestamps and diagnostic logs to operate the service. We do not intentionally log passwords, authorization credentials, verification/reset links or email message bodies.

Why we use information

We use this information to provide sign-in and recovery, verify email ownership, manage approved choir membership, store music and Mass programs, calculate choir-specific usage, prevent abuse, respond to support requests and maintain the service.

We do not sell personal data, run targeted advertising, or use account, choir or Google user data to train AI models. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

Who can see content

  • Signed-in app users can browse the shared hymn library of approved metadata, lyrics and PDF sheet music. Member-created private hymns are visible to their author and platform Admins. When a member submits a hymn, the selected choir’s planners can review it. Approval publishes the material for all signed-in users and choirs; private review feedback and the submission choir are not included in the published library.
  • Authorized choir members and platform Admins can access that choir’s Mass programs. Choir planners manage membership requests and settings.
  • Members can view their choir roster using usernames. Outsiders do not see the roster, email addresses or Masses. Public directory and code previews show only the choir name, description and member count when enabled.
  • The operator and authorized Admins can access information when needed to operate, support, secure or administer the service. We do not claim end-to-end encryption or that information is inaccessible to the operator.

Google Gmail API and account emails

The operator authorizes only gmail.send to send requested verification and recovery emails. We store the operator’s OAuth client credentials and refresh token privately on the backend. Short-lived access tokens are used in server memory. They are not given to app users or included in the frontend.

We send the recipient address and the necessary email subject, text and account-action link to Google for delivery. We do not request permission to read any user’s Gmail inbox, existing messages, contacts or calendar. Users do not authorize Google access to use Choralation.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only for the described account-email function, not advertising, resale or unrelated uses.

The operator can revoke the sending authorization in Google account connections and remove its stored credentials; this stops email sending. Delivery messages and copies in the sender’s Sent folder or the recipient’s mailbox follow the respective email provider’s retention and deletion settings. Revoking access does not delete those existing messages.

Service providers and location

Render hosts the application. Supabase stores the database and private PDF objects; PDF download access is controlled by the backend. Google handles outgoing Gmail delivery, and the recipient’s provider handles their mailbox. These providers process information needed to deliver their services, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Our hosting configuration targets Frankfurt, but provider operations, email routing and support can involve other countries. The operator is in Lebanon. We do not promise that all information remains in a single country.

Retention and deletion

We retain active account information and service content while needed to provide Choralation, maintain shared choir records, handle requests and protect the service. Leaving a choir does not automatically delete shared Mass programs or hymn materials. Requests can be made through the contact email above.

After ownership is confirmed, we can revoke access and remove personal account information. Shared choir programs and music may be retained without unnecessary account attribution. A last planner must arrange a replacement so that deleting an account does not leave a choir unmanaged. Private unpublished hymn copies and their files should be removed as part of an approved account-deletion request; shared approved hymns may be retained without unnecessary personal attribution. We will explain any retained information or limitation when responding.

Verification links expire after 24 hours; recovery links expire after one hour and are single-use. Expired security records are removed by an operator-run cleanup procedure after their retention window; this is not a continuously running deletion service. Technical logs follow provider retention and operational needs. Backups may retain earlier copies until their retention period ends; mailbox copies must be managed with the respective provider. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup or mailbox.

Cookies, security and your choices

We use essential session and CSRF cookies. The selected choir can be remembered in browser session storage. You can clear browser data, but doing so can sign you out and reset preferences. No advertising-cookie consent is required for these essential functions.

Hosted traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as hashes; verification and reset tokens are stored as hashes. Permissions, rate limits and private response caching controls help protect access. No system is perfectly secure. When enabled, a verification link can verify your email and sign you in automatically. Keep your password and account links private and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Password-reset responses use generic wording, but synchronous email delivery can make response times differ for registered addresses. Rate limits reduce abuse; this timing limitation has not been eliminated.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when our practices change and change the date above. Material changes to Google data use will be disclosed before that new use is introduced.
