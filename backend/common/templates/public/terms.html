Using Choralation

Choralation provides hymn organization and choir Mass planning. Keep your account details accurate and credentials private. Do not attempt to bypass permissions, impersonate others, send unsolicited messages through the service, or interfere with another user’s access.

Music and other content

You remain responsible for the material you contribute and for having the rights or permissions needed to upload and share it. Approved hymns, lyrics and uploaded PDFs are shared with signed-in users. Regular members can first save private hymns and request approval from a choir’s planners; submitting requests permission to publish that material to the shared library. Do not upload confidential personal documents. The operator may restrict content or accounts when necessary for security, rights complaints or service administration.

Choirs and availability

Joining a choir requires planner approval. Permissions determine who can view or change choir programs. The service is under active development and may be interrupted or changed; keep independent copies of important materials. We do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free availability.
