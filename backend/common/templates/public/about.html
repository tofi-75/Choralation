When email features are enabled, Choralation sends requested verification and password-reset messages from its operator’s Gmail account using Google’s send-only Gmail API permission. Only the operator connects that sending account. You do not connect your Google account, and you can register with an address from any email provider.

Choralation does not read inboxes, contacts or existing messages. It is an independent service and is not endorsed by Google.

Understand what you share

Hymns, lyrics and uploaded PDFs are shared with other signed-in users. Choir programs are available only to authorized choir members and platform Admins. Read the privacy policy and terms of use before adding content.
